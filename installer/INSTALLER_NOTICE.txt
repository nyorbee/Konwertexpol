Konwertexpol - what is installed
================================
Konwertexpol itself is free software under the MIT license (shown on the next page).

This package also contains programs made by other people. They are installed in the "tools" folder, are run as
separate programs, and keep their own licenses:

  FFmpeg ........................ GPLv3
  Pandoc ........................ GPL v2 or later
  Poppler ....................... GPL
  Ghostscript ................... AGPL (or Artifex commercial license)
  ImageMagick ................... ImageMagick License (permissive)
  qpdf, Tesseract OCR ........... Apache-2.0
  libjxl ........................ BSD-3-Clause
  pikepdf, ocrmypdf ............. MPL-2.0
  pdf2docx ...................... MIT
  PyMuPDF ....................... AGPL-3.0 (or Artifex commercial license)
  fpdf2, img2pdf ................ LGPL-3.0
  Pillow ........................ MIT-CMU
  Python ........................ PSF License
  other Python modules .......... mostly MIT / BSD / Apache-2.0

The license texts and the source of the GPL / AGPL programs are available from the authors of each program. See
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md (installed next to the program) for the details and links, and the license files inside the
tools folders. The list was compiled carefully but may be incomplete. It is not legal advice.
